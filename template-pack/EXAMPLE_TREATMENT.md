# Fictional example: Antigravity for Earthlings

An illustrative :30 footwear/comfort brand film. This is creative sample material, not an approved client treatment or product efficacy claim. All referenced media below are placeholders to be supplied or produced.

## Opening promise

A day keeps asking more of a New Yorker. The film expresses lightness through performance, rhythm and visual metaphor. Avoid depicting literal therapeutic outcomes without approved substantiation.

## Director's point of view

Feet are the rhythmic through-line. We return to motion at pavement level, then lift to the face: the emotional payoff. The smile survives the rush. A frown becomes a joke shared with a child.

## Mixed-media working boards

Moodboard: street photography, cool morning light, warm human faces, pavement textures, short camera-movement clips, percussive footstep audio. Each reference is labeled by purpose: borrow rhythm, borrow composition, or avoid styling.

Spatial scout: exported location mesh/point cloud, calibrated unit scale, saved camera positions for stairs and pavement. Link the scan to S02's setup notes. A scan informs access and coverage; the recce still verifies practical conditions.

Product staging: supplied product mesh with material reference photos. Save macro and hero cameras. If the true material cannot be reproduced accurately, use the model for blocking and a photograph for the presentation hero.

Sound board: script, three narration takes, footstep samples, temporary music and license status. All takes reference the same script revision. A selected take feeds both the treatment presentation and animatic.

Artwork: preserve the typography PSD; place its approved composite in the deck. Use the logo SVG for scale-independent display where supported. Attach the original sources for the design handoff.

## Client presentation

Six presentation frames: campaign promise; human story; visual language; storyboard sequence; sound demonstration; production approach and deliverables. Publish only selected frames and approved assets. Keep casting contacts, source licensing discussion and budget working notes on team boards.
